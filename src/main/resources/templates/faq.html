Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a ride?

Create an account, sign in, and go to Book Ride. Enter pickup and drop-off locations, date, time, and vehicle type.

How is the fare calculated?

Fares start at LKR 500 plus an estimated distance charge. Premium and XL vehicles have multipliers. Premium members get 10% off.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes. From your dashboard, cancel any confirmed booking before pickup time.

What payment methods are accepted?

Cash, card, and mobile payments are accepted. Set your preference in your profile.

Is TaxiGo available outside Colombo?

We primarily serve Greater Colombo and airport routes. Contact us for inter-city enquiries.
